# Brand assets

`reeb-icon.svg` is the editable project-supplied mascot. `reeb-icon-256.png` is its selected application export. `reeb-wordmark.svg` combines that icon with outlined Plus Jakarta Sans Bold lettering, so it needs no installed font.

The software's MIT license does not assert ownership or establish clearance of the mascot artwork. Separate artwork rights have not been documented. Retention of the supplied icon is a project-owner decision; resolve artwork redistribution rights before a public release.

Switch illustrations are original vector drawings of generic switch mechanisms. Their colors are illustrative rather than manufacturer measurements. The catalog illustrations are in `switches/generated/`.
